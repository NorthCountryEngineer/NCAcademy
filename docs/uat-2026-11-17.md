## Positive observations and limits

- Grade 3 local sign-in, enrollment and the nine-question preference intake completed. Intake is mandatory and may feel long for a child; this is a usability observation, not evidence of a malfunction.
- `3 groups with 4 blocks` produced an appropriate equal-groups hint without supplying the answer. A follow-up asking for a different explanation used a different analogous example (`2 × 5`).
- The compound-expression response completed within 6.5 seconds of the measured Send interaction. One other response was still pending at 6.8 seconds and was visible by the next observation at 20.9 seconds; that is an observation interval, not a precise server-latency measurement or a demonstrated latency regression.
- Worksheet publication remained private to the selected synthetic student. Existing real-student drafts and results were not modified.
- The published Grade 3 assignment accepted all six choices, correctly returned **4 of 6**, saved the work, and linked the two missed items to the approved area lesson. The subsequent lesson handoff and history worked.
- The Grade 10 Environmental Science class has no authored coursework. That empty state is accurate; it is not a failure to load assignments or a generated-workbook claim.
- Automated regression tests use synthetic records and mocked providers. Live manual UAT is recorded separately; no production model settings or privacy protections are weakened.

## Verification and disposition

The source changes include focused unit, integration, privacy-egress and interaction regressions for the findings above. An independent review checked worksheet mathematics, exact-source lesson excerpts, compiler task preservation and data boundaries. The complete frontend run passes 561 tests; its production build succeeds. Release completion additionally requires exact-commit CI, deployed artifact verification, unchanged-record/identity/policy checks and post-release Firefox UAT. Deployment receipts and test-account cleanup belong to the final operational acceptance report; this source report does not claim that a staged fix is already deployed.
