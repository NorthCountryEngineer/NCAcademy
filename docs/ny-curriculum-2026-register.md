## Implementation transitions

These are dated source findings. Later or proposed requirements are not treated as current mandates.

### Next Generation Algebra II assessment

Applies from: **2026-06**. Next Generation Algebra II instruction began September 2025 and its first Regents examination was June 2026. The last legacy 2011 Algebra II examination was January 2026. Algebra I and Geometry transitioned in June 2024 and June 2025 respectively. [Next Generation Mathematics Instruction and Assessment Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/next-gen-mathematics-instruction-assessment-timeline.pdf), [Mathematics Guidance and Resources](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/mathematics-guidance-resources), [Algebra II](https://www.nysed.gov/state-assessment/algebra-ii).

### Next Generation ELA Regents cutover

Applies from: **2026-06**. June 2026 is the first Next Generation ELA Regents administration; January 2026 was the last legacy administration. This is an assessment transition, not publication of a new annual curriculum. [New York State Next Generation English Language Arts Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-ela-standards.pdf), [Next Generation ELA Instruction and Assessment Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/next-gen-ela-instruction-assessment-timeline.pdf).

### World Languages revised standards reach grade 10

Applies from: **2026-09-01**. Revised 2021 standards are required for grade 10 instruction by September 1, 2026; the first required aligned Checkpoint B assessment is June 2027. Grade is not a universal proficiency level. [New York State Learning Standards for World Languages](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/world-languages/nys-learning-standards-for-world-languages-2021.pdf), [World Languages Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/world-languages/implementation-timeline), [Checkpoint B Assessment Guidance](https://www.nysed.gov/world-languages/checkpoint-b-assessment-guidance).

### World Languages grade 11 phase

Applies from: **2027-09-01**. Mandatory grade 11 revised-standard implementation begins after the 2026–27 year; schools may adopt earlier. Preserve the school’s actual version. [World Languages Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/world-languages/implementation-timeline).

### World Languages grade 12 phase

Applies from: **2028-09-01**. Mandatory grade 12 revised-standard implementation begins September 2028, completing K–12 alignment; do not represent it as universally completed in 2026–27. [World Languages Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/world-languages/implementation-timeline).

### ACCESS replaces annual NYSESLAT assessment

Applies from: **2027-spring**. Final NYSESLAT administration was spring 2026. First WIDA ACCESS and Alternate ACCESS administration is spring 2027. Instruction remains aligned to NY ALDs and Next Generation ELA; assessment adoption is not adoption of a replacement instructional curriculum. [Joining the WIDA Consortium and New English Language Proficiency Assessments](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/state-assessment/ny-wida-consortium-elp-assessments.pdf), [WIDA: New York State Assessment Information](https://www.nysed.gov/state-assessment/wida), [Academic and Linguistic Demands: Creating Access to Next Generation ELA for Linguistically Diverse Learners](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/bilingual-ed/academic-and-linguistic-demands-final-11-7-23-a.pdf), [2026 NYSESLAT School Administrator’s Manual](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/state-assessment/nyseslat-sam-2026.pdf).

### English-language screener transition follows ACCESS

Applies from: **2027-fall**. NYSITELL continues until WIDA screeners are implemented in fall 2027. Do not replace the 2026–27 intake assessment prematurely or invent a score conversion. [Joining the WIDA Consortium and New English Language Proficiency Assessments](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/state-assessment/ny-wida-consortium-elp-assessments.pdf).

### Personal finance instruction permanently adopted

Applies from: **2026-03-25**. The March 2026 amendment became permanently effective March 25, 2026. It creates a phased instructional requirement, not a new standalone diploma credit or new state learning standards set. [Personal Finance Education FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/personal-finance-education-faq).

### Middle and high school personal finance phase begins

Applies from: **2026-2027**. Implementation starts in the 5-8 and 9-12 bands in 2026-2027. Incoming grade 5 and grade 9 students must receive instruction by the end of those bands; students exiting a band during the initial year may not yet have received it. Schools choose delivery within each band. [Personal Finance Education FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/personal-finance-education-faq), [Personal Finance Topics and Grade-Band Learning Objectives](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/personal-finance-topics-grade-bands).

### Elementary personal finance phase follows

Applies from: **2027-2028**. Required K-4 implementation begins in 2027-2028. Catalog coverage in 2026-2027 supports planning or earlier local instruction and must not be presented as an already effective requirement in every elementary grade. [Personal Finance Education FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/personal-finance-education-faq), [Personal Finance Topics and Grade-Band Learning Objectives](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/personal-finance-topics-grade-bands).

### 2020 PE standards are the current full-implementation baseline

Applies from: **2023-2024**. NYSED identifies full implementation from fall 2023 onward. For 2026-2027, use the 2020 PE standards rather than the PE portion of the combined 1996 health/PE/FACS document. [New York State Physical Education Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/standards-instruction/new-york-physical-education-learning-standards-2020.pdf), [Physical Education Guidance and Resources](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/physical-education-guidance-resources).

### Middle-level CTE includes computer science

Applies from: **2022**. The official FAQ says computer science was added as a seventh CTE content area in 2022. Retain all seven current areas despite older prose referring to six; school-selected areas and appropriate certification still control local delivery. [New York State Middle Level CTE Requirement FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/career-technical-education/new-york-state-middle-level-cte-requirement-faq), [New York State Middle Level CTE Requirement](https://www.nysed.gov/career-technical-education/new-york-state-middle-level-cte-requirement).

### Grade 5 and grade 8 science assessment cutover

Applies from: **2024-spring**. New NYSP12SLS elementary/intermediate science tests first administered in spring 2024; science remains represented in every grade, not only assessment years. [Science Standards Development, Adoption and Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/curriculum-instruction/science-timeline.pdf).

### Biology and Earth/Space science assessment cutover

Applies from: **2025-06**. New Life Science: Biology and Earth and Space Sciences Regents exams began June 2025. Legacy Living Environment and Physical Setting/Earth Science ended June 2026. [Science Standards Development, Adoption and Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/curriculum-instruction/science-timeline.pdf), [Science FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/science-faq).

### Chemistry and Physics assessment transition

Applies from: **2026-06**. New Physical Science: Chemistry and Physics Regents exams began June 2026. Legacy Physical Setting/Chemistry and Physics continue through June 2027 for matching prior instruction; course/exam/reference-table versions must remain explicit. [Science Standards Development, Adoption and Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/curriculum-instruction/science-timeline.pdf), [Science FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/science-faq), [Frequently Asked Questions Related to Investigations for Regents Examinations in Science](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/state-assessment/faq-regents-science-investigations-updated-9-26.pdf).

### Full CSDF implementation

Applies from: **2024-09-01**. Full implementation began September 1, 2024. Students must achieve standards by the end of K–1, 2–3, 4–6, 7–8 and 9–12 bands; delivery and annual sequence remain local. [Computer Science and Digital Fluency FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/computer-science-digital-literacy-faq).

### Climate education regulation permanently effective

Applies from: **2026-03-25**. Permanent regulation effective March 25, 2026 with later phased instructional requirements. Early implementation is permitted; existing science climate content already applies. [Climate Education Instructional Requirement](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/climate-education), [Personal Finance Education and Climate Education Implementation Memorandum](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/standards-instruction/memo/signed-100.2-pfclimate-education-memo-march-2026-2.pdf).

### Climate education phase-in for middle and high school

Applies from: **2027-2028**. Beginning in 2027–28, instruction is required within grades 5–8 by the end of grade 8 and grades 9–12 by the end of grade 12; school district/charter verification is required during phase-in. [Climate Education Instructional Requirement](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/climate-education), [Personal Finance Education and Climate Education Implementation Memorandum](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/standards-instruction/memo/signed-100.2-pfclimate-education-memo-march-2026-2.pdf).

### Climate education phase-in for elementary school

Applies from: **2028-2029**. Beginning in 2028–29, instruction is required within K–4 by the end of grade 4; do not substitute a K–5 band or describe this as an already-required 2026–27 sequence. [Climate Education Instructional Requirement](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/climate-education), [Personal Finance Education and Climate Education Implementation Memorandum](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/standards-instruction/memo/signed-100.2-pfclimate-education-memo-march-2026-2.pdf).

## Review notes

- **ny-math-plus-is-not-course**: Plus standards may supplement high-school courses and prepare students for calculus, advanced statistics or discrete mathematics. They are not a separate course or a complete calculus curriculum. [New York State Next Generation Mathematics Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/standards-instruction/nys-next-generation-mathematics-p-12-standards.pdf).

- **ny-math-obsolete-timeline**: An April 2020 implementation timeline remains searchable but has obsolete dates. Use the January 2023 revision and current NYSED guidance. [Next Generation Mathematics Instruction and Assessment Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/next-gen-mathematics-instruction-assessment-timeline.pdf), [Mathematics Guidance and Resources](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/mathematics-guidance-resources).

- **ny-math-practices**: Mathematical reasoning, modeling, precise communication, tools and structural thinking run through every grade and course. Tutoring should elicit student reasoning rather than complete assessed work. [New York State Next Generation Mathematics Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/standards-instruction/nys-next-generation-mathematics-p-12-standards.pdf).

- **ny-math-accelerated-enrollment**: Schools may offer high-school mathematics before grade 9. The catalog grade scope describes content planning; it does not restrict a younger student from enrollment in a school-authorized advanced course. [Middle Level Education](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/middle-level-education).

- **standards_not_annual_curriculum**: 2026–27 labels applicability of reviewed authorities. New York has no single annually issued complete statewide teaching curriculum for all subjects. Schools select curriculum, texts, pacing and electives within requirements. [Early Learning Curriculum Guidance](https://www.nysed.gov/early-learning/curriculum), [Social Studies FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/social-studies-faq), [Resource Guides for School Success: The Prekindergarten Early Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/early-learning/pk-standards-resource-web-revised-2021.pdf).

- **scope_catalog_not_teaching_pack**: Short topic phrases are original scope summaries for planning and discovery. They are not full standard statements, numbered standards, approved student-facing lessons or evidence of complete corpus coverage. [New York State Next Generation English Language Arts Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-ela-standards.pdf), [New York State K–8 Social Studies Framework](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/standards-instruction/ss-framework-k-8a2.pdf), [New York State Grades 9–12 Social Studies Framework](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/standards-instruction/framework-9-12-with-2017-updates.pdf), [New York State Learning Standards for World Languages](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/world-languages/nys-learning-standards-for-world-languages-2021.pdf).

- **preserve_grade_band_and_proficiency**: ELA uses PK–8 grades and 9–10/11–12 bands; actual disciplinary-literacy tables use 6–8, 9–10 and 11–12; languages use checkpoints and language-specific proficiency. Do not fabricate a fixed annual sequence where the authority is banded or proficiency-based. [New York State Next Generation English Language Arts Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-ela-standards.pdf), [Next Generation Grades 6–12 Literacy Standards in History/Social Studies, Science and Technical Subjects](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-literacy-standards-grades-6-12.pdf), [World Languages Implementation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/world-languages/implementation-timeline).

- **disciplinary_literacy_intro_band_label**: The source introduction summarizes bands as 6–8 and 9–12, but actual reading/writing standard tables separately label 6–8, 9–10 and 11–12. This catalog follows the table labels and does not merge away the high-school progression. [Next Generation Grades 6–12 Literacy Standards in History/Social Studies, Science and Technical Subjects](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-literacy-standards-grades-6-12.pdf).

- **social_studies_embedded_legacy_literacy**: Social-studies framework PDFs retain older embedded Common Core literacy passages. Pair their subject content with the later 2017 Next Generation disciplinary literacy authority rather than silently treating the embedded text as current literacy alignment. [New York State K–8 Social Studies Framework](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/standards-instruction/ss-framework-k-8a2.pdf), [New York State Grades 9–12 Social Studies Framework](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/standards-instruction/framework-9-12-with-2017-updates.pdf), [Next Generation Grades 6–12 Literacy Standards in History/Social Studies, Science and Technical Subjects](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-literacy-standards-grades-6-12.pdf).

- **world_languages_topic_count_conflict**: The official themes page states four themes and 17 modern topics but separately claims 18 topics at Checkpoint C. The exact 2021 PDF table has 17 topics; retain the table as evidence and do not encode the inconsistent HTML count. [New York State Themes and Topics for World Languages](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/world-languages/nys-wl-themes-and-topics-2021.pdf), [World Languages Themes and Topics](https://www.nysed.gov/world-languages/themes-and-topics).

- **world_languages_track_numbering**: Modern standard 2 is interpersonal and 3 presentational; classical standard 2 is presentational and 3 interpersonal. Track-specific numbering must remain intact. [New York State Learning Standards for World Languages](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/world-languages/nys-learning-standards-for-world-languages-2021.pdf).

- **asl_proficiency_conflict**: The August 2021 category 1–2 proficiency document assigns ASL matching targets, while the April 2026 Checkpoint B page groups ASL with category 3–4. Require ASL-specific specialist review before assigning checkpoint targets or claiming complete proficiency coverage. [Proficiency Ranges and Performance Indicators for Modern Languages, Category 1–2](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/world-languages/proficiency-ranges-and-performance-indicators-for-modern-languages-cat-1-2_2.pdf), [Checkpoint B Assessment Guidance](https://www.nysed.gov/world-languages/checkpoint-b-assessment-guidance).

- **language_assessment_is_not_curriculum_adoption**: NYSED’s October 8, 2025 memo expressly preserves ALD and Next Generation ELA instruction while changing English-proficiency assessment. Do not infer adoption of WIDA ELD Standards as the school curriculum, replace the NY ALDs, or map old NYSESLAT levels to WIDA scores automatically. [Joining the WIDA Consortium and New English Language Proficiency Assessments](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/state-assessment/ny-wida-consortium-elp-assessments.pdf), [Academic and Linguistic Demands: Creating Access to Next Generation ELA for Linguistically Diverse Learners](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/programs/bilingual-ed/academic-and-linguistic-demands-final-11-7-23-a.pdf), [Units of Study Tables for English as a New Language and Bilingual Education Programs](https://www.nysed.gov/bilingual-ed/units-study-tables-english-new-language-enl-and-bilingual-education-programs).

- **media_and_library_guidance_status**: Library/media-literacy scope draws from existing literacy expectations plus guidance. Library/information-skills instruction is identified for grades 7 and 8, and the January 2025 toolkit is not an independently numbered new K–12 subject standard. [School Library Services FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/school-library-services-faq), [Media Literacy: A Toolkit for Educators](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/media-literacy-toolkit.pdf), [New York State Next Generation English Language Arts Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-ela-standards.pdf), [Next Generation Grades 6–12 Literacy Standards in History/Social Studies, Science and Technical Subjects](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-literacy-standards-grades-6-12.pdf).

- **local_elective_syllabus_required**: Humanities electives are optional school offerings. Transferable literacy skills do not establish a full statewide psychology, sociology, philosophy, writing, journalism or debate syllabus. Require a reviewed local syllabus and corpus; external AP/IB/college frameworks remain separate. [New York State Next Generation English Language Arts Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/sites/default/files/nys-next-generation-ela-standards.pdf), [Early Learning Curriculum Guidance](https://www.nysed.gov/early-learning/curriculum), [Social Studies FAQ](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/social-studies-faq).

- **ny_inspires_pending_diploma_rules**: The September 2026 timeline describes proposed diploma-assessment changes for fall 2027 and competency-based implementation for the 2029 entering cohort. It explicitly says existing graduation requirements remain until Board approval. Do not remove 2026–2027 requirements or treat the Portrait of a Graduate as a replacement subject syllabus. [NY Inspires Transformation Timeline](https://www.nysed.gov/ny-inspires/transformation-timeline).

- **ny_alternate_access_overlay**: Include accessible tutoring across the academic catalog. NYSAA/DLM is an eligibility-based alternate assessment pathway, not an automatic lower-grade curriculum or a diagnosis inferred from chat. A separately reviewed Essential Elements mapping and school-authorized individualized plan are needed before claiming alternate-pathway alignment. [New York State Alternate Assessment](https://www.nysed.gov/state-assessment/new-york-state-alternate-assessment).

- **ny-applied-cross-curricular-required-instruction**: School planning must include applicable highway/traffic safety, fire and arson prevention, injury/life safety and emergency-reporting education; citizenship, civility, anti-bullying/discrimination and safe Internet use; humane treatment of animals/birds in public elementary schools; and natural-resource conservation in public schools. These are cross-curricular instructional overlays, not newly invented standalone required courses. Drills, emergency practice and supervision remain outside chat-only tutoring. [General School Requirements Related to Standards and Instruction, Section 100.2](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/general-school-requirements-related-standards-and-instruction-section-1002).

- **ny-applied-scope-only**: This is a reviewed coverage and provenance catalog using original topic summaries. It is not a complete standards transcription, a state-issued curriculum, a human-approved teaching corpus or evidence that lessons are ready for student use. [About the P-12 Learning Standards](https://www.nysed.gov/standards-instruction/about-p-12-learning-standards).
